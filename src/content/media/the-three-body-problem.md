A sprawling first-contact novel set against the backdrop of the Cultural Revolution. Liu Cixin's ambition is staggering — the physics is grounded enough to feel plausible even when it isn't.

The game sequences are the best part. The rest can feel mechanical in translation, but the ideas carry it.
